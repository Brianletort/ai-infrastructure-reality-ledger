# Code of conduct

## Standard

Contributors must make this project a professional, evidence-led place to work. Be direct about
technical and factual disagreements without attacking people. Distinguish observed facts,
interpretation, and uncertainty. Accept correction when evidence changes.

Expected behavior includes:

- critique claims, code, methods, and sources rather than people;
- disclose conflicts, provenance, source rights, and material uncertainty;
- use inclusive language and respect different backgrounds and experience levels;
- keep review comments specific, actionable, and proportionate;
- protect private, confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate misrepresentation of sources, or knowingly contributing
restricted material.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, review, issue triage, project events, and public
representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue for conduct reports that contain personal, confidential, or
security-sensitive information. Use the private reporting path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
label the subject as a code-of-conduct report.

Maintainers will limit disclosure, acknowledge the report when a private channel exists, gather
relevant evidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and decide a proportionate response. Responses may
include a clarification, warning, temporary participation limit, removal of content, or permanent
exclusion. A maintainer named in a report must not adjudicate it.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)
but is written for this project's evidence, provenance, and privacy requirements.
