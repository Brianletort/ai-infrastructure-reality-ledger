# Launch narrative

## Forwardable summary

AI infrastructure reporting has a basic category problem. An announcement, a construction signal,
and operational capacity are not interchangeable, yet they are routinely agglomerated into one
number.

The AI Infrastructure Reality Ledger is an open-source attempt to make that distinction explicit.
It links claims to evidence, separates lifecycle states and timestamps, records source rights, and
preserves corrections rather than overwriting history.

The current beta is intentionally narrow. It contains six fixture-derived inventory records and 100
reviewed synthetic timelines. None is real market coverage. The point is to expose the method,
interface, and contribution controls before adding factual scope.

My read: the useful contribution is not another map. It is a defensible chain from visible claim to
source, review, uncertainty, and revision. The open question is whether the community finds that
discipline useful enough to improve source by source.

Start with the README, then inspect `/launch`, the methodology, and the contribution examples. The
most useful early contributions are narrow source reviews, correction packets, and synthetic
adapter fixtures.

## Short demo narration

This is the AI Infrastructure Reality Ledger. The persistent banner matters: every record in this
demo is synthetic and not public factual data.

The home page states the actual scope: six fixture-derived inventory records and 100 reviewed
synthetic timelines. No market coverage is claimed.

The globe starts with place, using generalized locations. Selecting a metro or event moves from the
map into a timeline. Announcement, construction, activation, contest, and correction remain
distinct.

## FAQ

### Is this a current inventory of AI data centers?

No. The checked-in inventory has six synthetic fixture-derived records. It is not a census or
market estimate.

### Are the timelines factual?

No. All 100 timelines are deterministic synthetic reviewed fixtures marked `NOT PUBLIC FACTUAL
DATA`.

### Why publish synthetic data?

It lets reviewers test the domain model, interface, correction path, and governance controls
without presenting unreviewed market claims as fact.

### What qualifies as activation evidence?

The synthetic method requires at least two independent signals, one authoritative signal, and one
non-imagery signal. That rule is a review policy demonstrated by fixtures, not a guarantee of
real-world accuracy.

### Can I submit a source?

Yes. Start with publisher, exact URL, authority, directness, cadence, terms, attribution, and
intended redistribution class. A public URL alone is not enough.

### Can I submit a correction?

Yes. Identify the exact record and field or claim, explain the proposed correction, and attach
supporting evidence. The project preserves prior history.

### Is the project production ready?

No. It has no public hosting, production ingestion, operational database, service level, or
representative midrange mobile measurement.

### Did the release gates pass?

The hardened launch-candidate rerun has 32 measured passes and 0 failures. The laptop real-GPU
gate passed under the stronger artifact contract; the overall decision remains inconclusive because
representative midrange mobile performance is unmeasured under the current evidence policy.

### Is the name cleared?

No. A current exact-name web and GitHub search found no direct collision, but that is not trademark
clearance. Branding confirmation remains an external-release approval.

## Maintainer response templates

### Source request: needs rights detail

Thanks. The source may be relevant, but the rights and intended use are not yet explicit. Please
add the accountable publisher, exact source URL, terms or license URL, required attribution, and
one proposed class: `republish`, `derived-only`, `link-only`, or `prohibited`. We will not ingest
source content until that review is complete.

### Source request: scope accepted

This is narrow enough to review. Acceptance means we will evaluate the source and fixture design;
it does not approve factual records or redistribution. Next step: add a synthetic fixture and the
expected normalized output without copying restricted source content.

### Correction: evidence needed

The disputed record is clear, but the proposed correction is not yet supportable. Please provide an
exact reference, publisher, publication date, retrieval date, and rationale connecting that
evidence to this entity and field. Do not include private or access-controlled material.

### Correction: queued for review

The packet is complete enough for review. We will assess source rights, entity match, evidence
strength, conflicts, and downstream impact. If accepted, the correction will supersede the prior
claim while preserving its history.

### Unsupported claim in discussion

That statement goes beyond the checked-in evidence. Please narrow it to what the cited source
supports or mark it as an inference. The beta dataset is synthetic and cannot support a market,
capacity, adoption, accuracy, or operating-performance conclusion.

### Security report redirect

Please remove security-sensitive detail from the public thread and use the private process in
`SECURITY.md`. Do not include credentials, personal data, exploit payloads against third parties,
or confidential system information.

### Contribution declined

We are declining this change because provenance or redistribution rights are not sufficiently
clear. That is a source-governance decision, not a judgment on the likely factual claim. A
link-only citation or a different approved source may still be viable.
