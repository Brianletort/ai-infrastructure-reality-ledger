# Clean-room provenance policy

## Purpose

Keep the Reality Ledger independently publishable and prevent confidential or incompatible material
from entering code, data, prompts, documentation, design, or assets.

## Required provenance record

Every substantive contribution must identify whether it is:

1. original work authored for this repository;
2. derived from a named public standard or public-domain source;
3. adapted from a named, license-compatible open-source work; or
4. generated with assistance, with the contributor responsible for verifying origin and rights.

Unverifiable provenance is not accepted.

## Prohibited inputs

- Magellan or Pawsey implementation files, schemas, prompts, data, styles, or assets;
- employer, customer, partner, or competitor-confidential information;
- access-controlled material obtained without explicit redistribution rights;
- code or content with incompatible or unknown licensing;
- personal data that is not necessary, lawful, and explicitly approved.

Similarity of public concepts does not permit copying implementation expression.

## Review controls

Reviewers check source attribution, license compatibility, data rights, and unexplained similarity.
Questionable material is quarantined or removed before merge. A provenance correction follows the
same append-only correction process as factual corrections and may require release withdrawal.

## Clearance record

Written employer/IP/disclosure/customer-competitor clearance was confirmed by the user on
2026-08-29. The project records that confirmation only; it does not claim to possess or independently
verify the clearance document.
